Quick fact

Three things worth knowing before you pour another.

01

A standard drink isn't what you think

12oz beer, 5oz wine, and a 1.5oz shot all contain about 14g of pure alcohol. A craft IPA or a mixed cocktail often counts as more.

02

Binge drinking has a definition

4+ drinks for women or 5+ for men within ~2 hours pushes most people over 0.08% BAC. That's the legal limit for driving — and well into impairment.

03

Pacing changes everything

Water between drinks, food in your stomach, and a one-drink-per-hour rhythm let your body keep up.
